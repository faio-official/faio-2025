Welcome to the FAIO 2025 Qualification round. Its six problems range from pen-and-paper statistics and probability to language identification, sensor classification, and counting shapes in images. Solve them in any order; each task is scored independently. Good luck.

- **1 — Statistics 101.** From a short list of numbers, compute the mean, the mode, and the median.
- **2 — Probability 101.** Find the probability of rolling at least one six when a die is thrown three times.
- **3 — Medicine.** For a rare disease and an imperfect test, use Bayes' theorem to find how likely a person with a positive result is actually sick.
- **4 — Who Speaks What?** Identify the language of each short message — Kazakh, Russian, or English.
- **5 — Can You Become an AI Yoga Instructor?** From wearable IMU sensor readings, decide whether each yoga repetition was performed correctly or incorrectly.
- **6 — Simple Objects.** Count exactly how many geometric figures (triangles, rectangles, circles) appear in each blueprint image.
